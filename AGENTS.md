# AGENTS.md

## Purpose
This repository contains the personal academic website of Kiyotada Mori.

## Content policy
- Keep the site bilingual in English and Japanese.
- Treat the current CV and user-provided updates as the source of truth for academic facts.
- Do not invent publications, awards, affiliations, funding, dates, metrics, or collaborators.
- Prefer concise research-oriented writing over promotional language.
- Keep publications in reverse chronological order within each category.
- Preserve exact paper titles and author ordering.
- Highlight first-author peer-reviewed international publications on the homepage.
- When adding a new item, update both English and Japanese text where applicable.

## Technical policy
- The site is intentionally static: HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.
- Avoid adding a framework unless there is a clear maintenance benefit.
- Keep the site responsive and accessible.
- Do not add tracking or analytics without explicit instruction.
- Before committing changes, check that internal anchors, external links, and the language toggle work.
- Keep GitHub Pages compatibility.

## Main files
- `index.html`: all website content
- `styles.css`: visual design
- `script.js`: bilingual toggle and small client-side behavior
