Response Prefetching応答プリフェッチ

Generating candidate responses from partial utterances so a dialogue system can reduce perceived latency without sacrificing response quality.

部分発話から応答候補を先行生成し、品質を保ちながら体感遅延を短縮する研究です。

Selective Listening選択的聴取

Studying what people actually hear during interaction to rethink ASR evaluation for spoken dialogue systems.
